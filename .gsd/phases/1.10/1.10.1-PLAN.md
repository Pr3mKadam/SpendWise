# Plan 1.10.1: "Signal" — Typographic Command-Center Dashboard

## Objective
Replace the cluttered card-grid dashboard with a radically minimal, editorial-style layout
called "Signal". The design borrows from Bloomberg terminals, Vercel's dashboard, and
broadsheet newspaper layouts. No traditional card shadows — typography and 1px hairlines
carry all the visual hierarchy. The result feels like a high-end trading room meets editorial design.

## Visual Language Rules
- Background: radial gradient mesh `#060b18 → #0a1220 → #060b18`
- All text on dark bg — white primary, slate-400 for muted labels
- Accent: `#2dd4bf` (teal) for income / positive, `#f87171` (red) for expenses
- NO `card` class, NO `box-shadow`, NO `border-radius` on section containers
- Section separators: `border-t border-white/[0.07]` (1px hairline)
- Balance number: `font-size: clamp(52px, 8vw, 88px)`, `font-weight: 900`
- Vitals labels: `font-size: 9px`, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`, uppercase, monospace
- Vitals values: `font-size: 20px`, `font-weight: 700`, tabular-nums
- Transaction list: left-aligned date+merchant, right-aligned amount, `font-family: monospace`
